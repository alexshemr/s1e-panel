Your Aqara S1E,
a Home Assistant touch panel

S1E Panel is custom firmware for the Aqara Magic Switch S1E. It replaces the factory interface with a fast, native dashboard for Home Assistant: your lights, curtains, climate devices and sensors — not only Aqara's — on a responsive 480 × 480 touch screen.

Why S1E Panel

Built for Home Assistant, designed for a wall.

A real Home Assistant panel

Lights, curtains and roller blinds, thermostats, humidifiers, fans, breezers, room climate, switches, scenes and scripts, helpers, weather, media and a 3D printer card.

Smooth and alive

Native LVGL drawing and swipeable pages. The lamp glows in its colour, the curtain moves in a window with the real sky, the fan spins at its real speed.

Arranged your way

Several pages on an 8 × 8 grid, cards in up to 11 sizes, your colours, icons, captions and the device modes you use.

Edit from any browser

A web editor on the panel itself with a live preview, plus adding and moving cards right on the touch screen.

Screensavers and messages

Clock, live weather, home readings or your photos — or a shuffle. Home Assistant sends messages with forecasts or a doorbell camera photo.

No tokens, safe updates

A companion integration pairs with a tap on the panel. Updates come over Wi-Fi, signed, with automatic rollback.

Install on your Aqara S1E

Once through the service console, then every update over Wi-Fi.

  1. Check the model: Aqara Magic Switch S1E, NPD1121WSK01.
  2. Get console access to the panel (UART) — as documented by oopuuu.
  3. Run the installer with the first-install bundle from the latest release. It test-starts S1E Panel before making it permanent.
  4. Connect Home Assistant with the S1E Panel integration (HACS) and add your first card.
